# Hive Basecamp

The section of the blog app where people brand new to Hive land, and where
established Hive users go to meet them. Not part of Denser as shipped.

This file is the glossary only — what words mean here, and which words we have
agreed not to use. No implementation details, no plans, no decisions about how
anything is built.

## Who is here

**New user**:
An account under a year old. A fact about the account's age, nothing more — it
says nothing about whether the person knows Basecamp exists or has used it.
_Avoid_: Newcomer, newbie, newb, arrival

**OG user**:
An account a year old or older. The counterpart to new user, and the same kind
of word: an age fact, drawn at exactly the same line. Elsewhere on Hive "OG"
usually means someone who has been here for years and years; here it means only
that the account has passed a year. If a further band is ever needed, it can be
added then.

**Interests on record**:
The up-to-five topics a person has publicly declared. There is nothing to join
and no membership to hold — declaring interests only puts those topics on
public record so a guide can find a match.

**Curator**:
Anyone forming a judgement about an account by looking at it. It sits above
guide, supporter and patroller — those are what you do next, curating is the act
of looking and weighing. A Hive-wide word, used here unchanged.

Not an OG-only word. A new user judging accounts in a game is curating exactly
as an OG user reading a card is. Basecamp sets out to make OG users better
curators, but it makes curators of new users too, and the word must stay open
enough to hold both.

## Ways to help

**Help**:
The umbrella word, deliberately left wide. It covers everything an established
user might come here to do, and is never used as the name of any one activity.

**Guide**:
Someone who has publicly offered to show one new user around, matched by shared
interests. Chosen over "mentor" because it is the lighter word — a guide walks
you somewhere, a mentor is a relationship — and more people will offer.
_Avoid_: Mentor, buddy, sponsor

**Support**:
Backing an individual new user's post, either by upvoting it or by replying to
it. On Hive an upvote is money, so support is usually read as financial, but a
reply is a real form of support too and both belong under the word.
_Avoid_: Browse

**Reply**:
A comment written on someone's post. The word for the act, in every context.

**Patrol**:
Helping the Hive ecosystem by spotting bots, scammers, spammers and blatant
extractors. The four Puppet Patrol games are this, in game form.

## What a postcard shows

**Postcard**:
Everything shown for one new user in the feed — what they wrote, plus all the
information we display about them. It is made of two halves, named below.

**Post**:
The thing the person actually wrote and published to Hive.

**Card**:
The information we show about that person alongside their post — the rings, the
readouts, and whatever else gets added. Two people read it: a curator deciding
whether to support them, and the person themselves, seeing how they come across.
A new user changing what their card says by going and doing things on Hive is the
point of Basecamp, not a side effect.

## Talking about the work

**Overview chart**:
The wayfinder planning file for an effort: what is decided, what is still open,
and its tickets. "Chart" or "overview" alone means this. Never call it the map.
The file on disk stays `map.md` because the skill looks for that name.

**Game map**:
The world you fly over in H.I.V.E.R. When "map" is said on its own, it means this.

## Making the game

**Element**:
One thing players enjoy doing or overcoming in a game, and come back for.
Exploring, a battle, a weapon that breaks without warning. Bryan's word.
_Avoid_: mechanism, mechanic, loop

**Hive truth**:
Something everyone on Hive knows or feels. Nobody wants a downvote. The DHF is
money. Bots. Witness votes.

**Match**:
One element laid over one Hive truth, made into a piece of the game.
_Avoid_: overlay (as a noun), mapping

## In the game

**Round**:
One 30-minute game. The world is built again from the chain when a new round
starts. The code says "window" for the same thing; we say round.

**Cemented**:
What stays from round to round when you keep playing. Its opposite is
per-round: gone when the round ends.

**Phase**:
A stretch of adventure mode that ends in a lock-in, where what you have becomes
cemented. Not used for moods of play (exploring, fighting); those are elements.
