# How far a loss can go

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Today three hits send the bug home and drop carried tokens. Once found things have value, how far can a loss go?

## Answer

Per mode. Explore mode soft, as now; likely the same for curation and front-end modes. Adventure mode closer to hard, with cemented phases: pass a phase and what you had is locked in; lose and you drop back to the last cemented phase, not to zero; then collect again toward the next lock-in. Bryan asked for real examples. Found: Dark Souls bonfires (return to the last bonfire, drop what you carried, keep levels and items); Hades and Dead Cells (a run's gains are lost, upgrades bought between runs stay); Hollow Knight benches; Mario mid-level checkpoint flags; classic Zelda (hearts and key items permanent, death restarts you at the dungeon entrance). His shape is roguelite meta-progression plus checkpoints. Details: ticket 24.
