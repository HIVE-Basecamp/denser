Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

How do H.I.V.E.R. and the Basecamp postcard relate? Does anything done in the game show on the card?

## Answer

Bryan: "The game feeds the card. The card feeds the game." Keep that. A space or illustration on the card connected to the game is fine; players will do tasks and win challenges or phases in the game, and the card may show that engagement. Playing the patrol games may open something in the game. What each looks like: to be determined. "I would like that door to remain open." The line "game progress never becomes card progress" was an earlier session's idea, never his, and is dropped. `docs/basecamp-context.md` already lets a game award Basecamp progress through the task action.
