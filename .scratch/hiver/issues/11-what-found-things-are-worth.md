# What found things are worth

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Tokens are a counter, gems do nothing, helmets extend the hop. Should things found in the game buy only capacity and looks, never power?

## Answer

Partly. Yes: tokens, gems, helmets and everything out there to find should have value inside the game; buy skins, change colours. No to "never power": in the adventure mode, beating enemies and chopping the dragon heads, you may need to collect things that give damage or extra speed against an enemy. Not one or the other. Roaming and ignoring the collecting must still work. Also wanted: a Zelda-style pop-up, a card or dashboard of what the player has collected, strength, weapons (ticket 21). Fog: games within games, winning money inside the game then a rug pull or a token going to zero; keep close to Hive lore and real crypto experience. Prices and powers: ticket 25.
