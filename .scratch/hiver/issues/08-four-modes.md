# Four modes

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

How does the game show the player what there is to do, without a checklist?

## Answer

Bryan's shape, like Zelda: you run into a thing or a place and it gives you a challenge. Land at Basecamp, some kind of "welcome to Basecamp", then pick a mode. Four modes, his words: (1) open explore: roam, not trying to do anything; (2) curation: focus on engaging with live posts, several kinds of missions offered here, this mode fills the game-linked part of the card; (3) adventure: the fun and inner challenges of the game, less touching the real chain, enemies, bring one weapon, battle one of Justin's minions; (4) front end: the tools linked to hive.blog stand out on the map. What each mode holds: ticket 16.
