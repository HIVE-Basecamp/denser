## Question

What makes people come back to adventure and exploration games, and which of those mechanisms fit a world rebuilt every 30 minutes from live Hive data?

## Answer

See `research/01-adventure-game-return-loops.md`. Twelve mechanisms with sources and PROPOSALs. Gaps found in H.I.V.E.R.: nothing records what the player learned about Hive; the calendar reaches only one day; connection to people happens only by leaving the game.
