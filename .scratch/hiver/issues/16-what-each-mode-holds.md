# What each mode holds, and the welcome at Basecamp

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Four modes are settled (ticket 08). What does each one hold, how does the player pick one, and what is the "welcome to Basecamp" when you land?

Open explore, curation, adventure, front end. Which HUD elements change per mode. Whether a mode can be switched mid-window. Whether the map draws differently per mode (front end: hive.blog tools stand out).

## Answer

Bryan, 2026-09-14, grilled on four questions, with what was on screen at the
time put in front of him first.

### The correction that matters more than the four answers

I asked which half adventure mode was: the fiction with enemies, or the
real-chain challenges. Wrong question, asked in the wrong spirit.

"You're taking what I would refer to as brainstorming and me having ideas and
missing larger picture... I'm trying to give mechanisms and fun elements to
add to the game, and you're looking for rules, and you're looking for literal
things. And I'm not really at that point... You always want to solve or create
a plan or make a rule, but we're still in that fertile decision making the fun
bit where you get to decide and create and brainstorm."

And the frame to hold instead: "build a world with many aspects and, again,
think Zelda. Think gameplay in explore adventure games... we're trying to
match the lores." He asked where the tickets went that were meant to find real
connections between Zelda-style adventure games and Hive lore. They are
ticket 33, the match table, still open.

His ideas from this session are in `.scratch/hiver/ideas.md`, dated, as ideas.

### 1 — What is adventure mode? BOTH, and the question was too small

Real-chain challenges are important AND the game side is: "game adventures and
purposes and challenges and enemies and battles, we should have more
interactions."

- Not the wrong half built first: "not specifically, but you'd lost the bigger
  picture point."
- The shape he wants more of: "The game should be about encountering something
  maybe being able to skip, but maybe some things can't be skipped. Maybe
  you're approached and you have to battle or you are brought into one of these
  JSON boxes."
- A gap he named: "the boxes just currently have no purpose. You should open
  these boxes or go into these boxes."
- An idea he had mid-answer, the golden ticket: one item hidden behind one of
  the live posts in the challenge, only one in the whole game, everyone hunting
  it, and reaching it means really acting on that post. What it is, later.

### 2 — Curation mode: light is fine

"It's fine if it's not doing too terribly much. People choose to just kind of
go." What he does want is more feeling on the trail, through colour:

- A post with no votes yet carries its own colour.
- A post with no comments carries its own colour.
- Posts from new users (Basecamp, under 365 days) are their own coloured trail.
- A first ever post pops confetti around it ON THE MAP, the way it does in the
  feed.
- Later, not now: trails for posts by people holding a certain badge; and
  rewards or reasons to choose curation at all.

"Doing a few of those little highlight things that gives more of a feeling to
the curation trail. That's enough for now."

### 3 — Switching mode mid-round: "keep it free for now"

No commitment, no penalty, no loss on switching. As it already behaves.

### 4 — Does the map change per mode? Yes, by dimming

"You can change them to slightly more dim if not the focus of the mode." So a
mode lifts its own things by letting everything else fall back, rather than by
adding more glow.
