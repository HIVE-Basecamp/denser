Bryan, 2026-09-14, closing this down before it started: "before anything gets
deployed, we will review. And when I say we, it'll be me and other Hybians.
We'll review what's acceptable and what's not acceptable. I won't even need

So this ticket is not a conversation to have with Claude. It is the agenda for
a human review that happens before a deploy, recorded so it is not lost.

## What he already said about it

- **No single tool calls anyone bad.** The judgement comes out of many real
  people using the tools: "after people begin to play these games, do their
  investigations, publish comments... a mass or swarm style of hive watchers,
  helping to protect the chain through games and through the users that are
  using and care about Hive."
- **Once enough hoops are passed, a badge may need a human to award it.**
  "There may very well be a final point where there's a human in the loop...
  maybe the only way a badge can get awarded will be by a human saying, yes,
  award the badge." Explicitly not settled: "I'm not even saying that it has
  to be a human in the loop. I'm just saying maybe it will be."
- **Why the badges exist at all**, in one sentence: retention, plus "give
  confidence to OG users that they can vote for this new user because they've
  been vetted or they have these badges. And for us to be able to catch the
  bad actors in the bot farms."
- **Nothing can reach the chain without his own authentication.** "The only
  way it's going on chain is if my user authentication allows that post or
  that thing to go on chain, and it's gonna be me doing it. There's nothing
  you currently can do that automates something like that." A tool that
  prepares something for him to publish is therefore fine to build.
- **If an agent ever gets its own signing account**, gates get discussed then:
  "let's just cross that bridge when we actually fucking arrive there."

## The agenda, for that review

- What hoops earn a positive badge, and what a curator may assume from it.
- What amount of agreement turns swarm findings into a public label.
- Which outside onboarding and policing projects we accept findings from.
- Appeal: who hears it, what a lifted label leaves behind.
- Whether a human awards each badge, or only some.
