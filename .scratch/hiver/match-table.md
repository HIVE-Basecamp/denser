# Part 1 — The elements. Are they true?

An element is one thing players enjoy in a game and come back for (CONTEXT.md).
For each: what it is, where it came from, and how sure I am.

**E1 The far-off thing you can see**
Something you cannot get to yet. You go there later.
From: `research/00`, the triangle rule and the climb-and-look loop.
Bryan, 2026-09-15, reshaped it. This is an **adventure mode** element. In
explore mode you can hop to most places, so it does not apply there. And the
gate is not distance. His words: "maybe it means like you have to do or have
some things". A second sense he added: something so far off "you don't even
quite realize it's there yet" — beyond awareness, not just beyond reach.
Sure? Yes, with that reshape. The first draft confused the map view with the
field view; the mode split answers it.

**E2 Secrets behind ordinary walls**
Anything might be hidden anywhere, so poking at a dull thing is worth a try.
From: `research/01`.
Sure? Yes for games. Untested for ours: we have few dull things to poke.

**E3 Reward for wandering off**
You go the wrong way on a whim and it pays out.
From: `research/00` — Nintendo moved the towers so players got sidetracked, and
baited the space between landmarks.
Sure? Yes. This is the strongest single finding in the Zelda research.

**E4 Only one of them exists**
One prize. Everybody hunting it.
From: Bryan, 2026-09-14, the golden ticket.
Sure? It is Bryan's, so it stays. Note it is a competition element, not an
exploring one, and competition needs other live players to bite.

**E5 The thing you love breaks without warning**
Your best weapon snaps. You get careful. You hoard the good one.
From: Bryan, 2026-09-06, his own Zelda example.
Sure? Yes as a feeling. Risky for us: it needs you to own things first, and
today the bug owns almost nothing.

**E6 Losing time, never your progress**
Failing costs a walk back, not the things you earned.
From: `research/01`.
Sure? Yes, and it already describes the game: no creature can kill the bug.

**E7 Going back with new legs**
A place that stopped you is easy now. You feel the change in yourself.
From: `research/01`.
Sure? Yes in games. For us it needs cemented things across rounds, so it
depends on ticket 24.

**E8 The world moved without you**
You come back and the place changed on its own.
From: `research/01`.
Sure? Yes, and it is free for us: the chain moves whether we play or not.

**E9 The thing that only happens now**
A festival, a clock, a window. Miss it and it is gone.
From: `research/01`.
Sure? Yes. The round clock already does it.

**E10 Traces of other players**
Signs someone stood here. No talking.
From: `research/00`, the Journey finding, which the notes call the strongest.
Sure? Yes, and it is the one place our game beats a normal game: the stranger
is a real account.

**E11 The place is yours**
A look, a board, a home nobody else's resembles. No advantage.
From: `research/01`, and `research/00`'s Korok discipline — cosmetics, never
power.
Sure? Yes.

**E12 Learning is the treasure**
What you walk away with is understanding. Nobody can take it off you.
From: `research/01`.
Sure? Yes, and it is the point of the whole project. But it is not really an
element like the others. It is a result. It may not belong in this list.

**Elements that matched nothing**
A companion you would be sorry to lose. A house that grows over months. Being
allowed to be bad at it, with no timer and no score. Beauty that stops you.

# Part 2 — The Hive truths. Are they true?

A Hive truth is something everyone on Hive knows or feels (CONTEXT.md). All ten
came out of `research/02`. The short names are the agent's. Rename any of them.

**T1 The fund pays the most-voted, not the best**
The DHF holds money. Each round it pays the proposals with the most votes. A
vote counts by stake, not by head.
Named "pays the popular" in the first draft. Bryan: the word is wrong, it
sounds like a count of people. It is a count of money. Renamed 2026-09-15.
True? Yes, and Bryan already called it "the real truth of Hive".

**T2 Nobody wants a downvote**
Anyone can downvote anything. There is no appeal, only argument.
True? Yes. Also a sore spot: half the chain calls it the immune system, half
calls it bullying.

**T3 Stake is voice**
How much you have locked up decides how loud your vote is.
True? Yes, and it is the hardest thing about Hive to defend to an outsider.

**T4 Three seconds, forever**
A block lands every three seconds whether anyone posts or not.
True? Yes. It is also the heartbeat in the lore bible.

**T5 Your vote is worth more if you waited**
Half of every reward goes to the voters. So when you vote, and who follows you,
is worth real money.
True? Yes, but it is the most technical of the ten. A new user does not feel it
until someone explains it.

**T6 Nothing can be unwritten**
Once it is in a block it stays. Deleting a post only hides it from the website.
True? Yes.

**T7 The community forked away**
In 2020 a buyer took the old chain with bought stake. The community copied
everything and left.
True? Yes, and it is already the spine of the lore bible.

**T8 The fish tell your size**
Redfish, minnow, dolphin, orca, whale. Everyone gets sorted by stake, and
everyone knows the words.
True? Yes, and it is folklore, not mechanism. That may make it the friendliest
of the ten.

**T9 Power Up Day**
On the 1st people lock coin up together and post about it. Nobody told them to.
True? Yes. Ticket 18 is parked, so nothing here is near building.

**T10 Spotting fakes is the reflex**
Twenty sock-puppet servers took the thrones once. The community never stopped
looking.
True? Yes, and it is the reflex Basecamp exists to serve.

**Truths that matched nothing**
Self-voting and rings of accounts. Cashing out. The volunteer police. Trending
being buyable for two years. Flag wars that ran for months. Naming the 2020
puppet accounts.
These are the sore spots. Nothing clicked, and that may be the point.

# Part 3 — The matches
